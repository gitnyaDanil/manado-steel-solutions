Future Improvements

1. Make Contact Easier
Your best business goal is getting people to contact the workshop.

Add or improve:

Floating WhatsApp button
Click-to-call button
“Kirim Pesan” form that opens WhatsApp with prefilled text
Clear CTA on every page
Right now the contact form should probably be changed so it actually sends users to WhatsApp instead of only showing a success toast.

2. Add Google Analytics
Search Console shows Google search traffic, but not all visitors. Add GA4 so you can see:

how many people visit
which pages they open
mobile vs desktop
where visitors come from
how many click WhatsApp
Then track WhatsApp clicks as an event.

3. Improve Local SEO
Add more search-friendly text for Manado services:

Jasa bubut Manado
Jasa las Manado
Bengkel korter Manado
Perbaikan crankshaft Manado
Bengkel alat berat Manado
Asphalt sprayer Sulawesi Utara
Also create/claim a Google Business Profile. For a local workshop, this may matter more than normal Google ranking.

4. Add Trust Content
Customers need confidence before calling.

Add:

Before/after repair photos
Short customer testimonials
“Sejak 1970” story section
Equipment/machine photos
Quality testing explanation
Common jobs handled
5. Improve Performance
The site uses many workshop photos. Compressing images can make mobile loading much faster.

Target:

WebP/AVIF images
lazy loading gallery images
smaller mobile image sizes
6. Add a Sitemap
Add:

/sitemap.xml
/robots.txt
Then submit sitemap in Google Search Console. This helps Google discover /services, /gallery, and /contact.

7. Polish Mobile Experience
Most customers will probably visit from phones. Check:

button sizes
WhatsApp visibility
text readability
gallery loading speed
contact info above the fold
